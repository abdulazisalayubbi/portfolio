Publication

Conference paper · IEEE · 2025

Comparative Performance Analysis of GetX and MobX in Flutter-Based Job Finder Application

ICoSEIT 2025 · Bandung, Indonesia · Abdul Azis Al Ayubbi, Sri Widowati, Monterico Adrian

I compared GetX and MobX state management in a Flutter job finder app built on the USAJobs API, profiling six interaction scenarios with 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 entries. MobX was more memory-efficient and had steadier CPU use, especially as the data grew.
